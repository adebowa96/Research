Menstrual Cycle, Stress, and Sleep in Wearable Data

Two analyses of thousands of daily Fitbit records from the public mcPHASES dataset, using linear mixed-effects models for repeated measures. Fertility and luteal phases showed more physical signs of stress, while daily physical activity was not associated with next-night sleep quality.

Data analysis · HLTH 501 Biostatistics · 2024

Cardiovascular Risk Factors in the Framingham Heart Study

I analyzed real, anonymized cohort data on 4,434 Framingham participants: describing the sample, comparing men and women, modeling heart rate with simple and multivariable regression, and testing which factors differ in people with coronary heart disease. Adjustment showed that the apparent link between diabetes and heart rate was mostly explained by glucose.

Research design & evidence synthesis

Mini systematic review · HLTH 625 Prevention and Control of Infectious Disease · 2025

Antibiotic Therapy for Buruli Ulcer in Ghana

I searched six databases following PRISMA guidelines, screened 126 records, and synthesized nine studies on treating this neglected tropical disease. The evidence shows oral rifampicin–clarithromycin heals over 90% of cases with recurrence below 3%, while emerging clarithromycin resistance calls for continued surveillance.

Research proposal · HLTH 511 Research Methods in Public Health · 2025

Stress, Anxiety, and Social Support Among Graduate Students: A Cross-Sectional Study Design

I designed a study to compare depression, anxiety, and stress between international and domestic graduate students, and to test whether perceived social support and campus services explain the differences. The design covers sampling and sample-size planning, validated instruments (DASS-21, MSPSS), confounders, a regression and moderation analysis plan, missing-data handling, and research ethics.

Intervention proposal · HLTH 509 Social & Behavioral Theory · 2025

Reducing Stress-Related Hypertension Among Police Officers in Ibadan, Nigeria

I designed a workplace intervention for police officers facing chronic occupational stress. It uses a PRECEDE needs assessment, ties every activity to Social Cognitive Theory or the IMB model, and includes a logic model, a stakeholder coalition, a pre/post evaluation plan, and policy recommendations.
