Research proposal · HLTH 511 Research Methods in Public Health · 2025

Stress, Anxiety, and Social Support Among Graduate Students: A Cross-Sectional Study Design

I designed a study to compare depression, anxiety, and stress between international and domestic graduate students, and to test whether perceived social support and campus services explain the differences. The design covers sampling, a power calculation, validated instruments (DASS-21, MSPSS), confounder adjustment, a regression and moderation analysis plan, missing-data handling, and research ethics.

Mini systematic review · HLTH 625 Prevention and Control of Infectious Disease · 2025

Antibiotic Therapy for Buruli Ulcer in Ghana

I searched six databases following PRISMA guidelines, screened 126 records, and synthesized nine studies on treating this neglected tropical disease. The evidence shows oral rifampicin–clarithromycin heals over 90% of cases with recurrence below 3%, while emerging clarithromycin resistance calls for continued surveillance.

  • PRISMA
  • Risk of bias
