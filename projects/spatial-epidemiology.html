Mapping the 1854 Soho Cholera Outbreak

A heat map in ArcGIS Online that recreates John Snow's classic investigation, showing how spatial analysis can point to the source of an outbreak.

Course
HLTH 652 Data Visualization & Spatial Analysis
Tool
ArcGIS Online
Layers
Cholera deaths, water pumps, Snow's 1854 map
Date
April 2026

What the map shows

The most intense red and yellow areas sit in one central block, and density fades with distance. Cases were not scattered at random: they cluster around a single water pump. That points to a localized source of exposure, contaminated water, the same conclusion Snow reached in 1854.

Why a heat map instead of points?

  • Clarity: hundreds of overlapping points become a continuous surface that shows where cases are dense.
  • Interpretability: hotspots stand out at a glance, which helps target interventions.
  • Trade-off: smoothing hides individual cases, so the point layer is still useful for exact locations.
